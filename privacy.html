This policy explains data used by Landing Lab: Flying Car on iOS, and the differences in its web and desktop versions.

Data on your device

The game stores progress, medals, best scores, paint, tutorial completion, preferences and pending scores locally. It does not request your camera, microphone, photo library, contacts or GPS location.

Optional Game Center

You can play as a guest without signing in. If you choose Game Center, Apple supplies a game-specific player identifier and public nickname. The game submits eligible scores to Apple and retrieves rankings. Other players can see your Game Center nickname, rank and score. The game does not receive your Apple password or email address. These records support authentication and leaderboards.

Optional iCloud saves

When Game Center is connected and iCloud is available, Apple iCloud key-value storage synchronizes mission progress, medals, best scores, paint and tutorial completion. Saves use a hashed Game Center profile identifier and a random device snapshot identifier. The device vendor identifier is used only locally and is not uploaded. Language and sound preferences do not sync. Game Center and iCloud use accounts configured on your device and may use different Apple accounts.

Providers and tracking

The iOS game uses Apple Game Center and iCloud rather than Unity anonymous login for these features. There are no ads, in-app purchases or cross-app advertising tracking. We do not operate a separate player-account database. Apple processes its services under Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Web and desktop versions

Those versions use Unity anonymous authentication and Unity Leaderboards for anonymous player identifiers, public call signs and eligible scores. Unity may process network information and service diagnostics to operate them. See Unity’s Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. The iOS game does not initiate that Unity sign-in flow.

Your choices and retention

Select guest play in the game’s Player account screen to stop using Game Center rankings and iCloud synchronization in the game. Switching profiles preserves previous records. Deleting the app removes its local data but does not automatically remove Apple-hosted scores or cloud saves; reinstalling and reconnecting may restore them.

Game Center and iCloud accounts are managed by Apple; the game does not create a separate account. For Apple-hosted data and account controls, use Apple’s Data and Privacy portal or Apple Support. We cannot access your private iCloud save store or promise to erase Apple-managed records through a support email. Contact us for questions about data used by the game. Records remain on your device or with the provider until removed through the applicable controls.

Support and this website

If you email support, we receive your address and any information you include. We use it to answer and resolve your request, retain it only as needed for support and applicable obligations, and accept access or deletion requests at the contact below. This website has no advertising or analytics scripts; its hosting provider may process standard request information to serve and secure the site. The language selector stores only a language preference in your browser.

Children

The game is not submitted to the App Store Kids category. It does not ask players for their age or contact information. Optional Apple services are subject to Apple’s account and family controls. If you believe a child has sent personal information to our support address, contact us to request removal.

本政策说明 Landing Lab：飞行跑车 iOS 版使用的数据，以及网页和桌面版本的区别。

本机数据

游戏在本机保存进度、奖牌、最高分、车漆、教程完成情况、偏好设置和待上传分数。不请求访问摄像头、麦克风、相册、通讯录或 GPS 位置。

可选 Game Center

玩家可选择游客，无需登录。选择 Game Center 后，Apple 提供游戏专属玩家标识及公开昵称。游戏向 Apple 提交符合条件的分数并获取排名；其他玩家可以看到你的 Game Center 昵称、排名和分数。游戏不会获得 Apple 密码或邮箱。这些记录用于身份识别和排行榜。

可选 iCloud 存档

连接 Game Center 且 iCloud 可用时，游戏通过 Apple iCloud 键值存储同步任务进度、奖牌、最高分、车漆和教程完成情况。存档使用经过哈希处理的 Game Center 身份标识和随机设备存档标识。设备供应商标识仅在本机使用，不上传。语言和声音设置不参与同步。Game Center 与 iCloud 使用设备设置中的账户，二者可能是不同 Apple 账户。

服务提供方与追踪

iOS 版使用 Apple Game Center 和 iCloud，而非 Unity 匿名登录实现这些功能。无广告、无应用内购买，也不进行跨应用广告追踪。我们不运营独立玩家账户数据库。Apple 根据其隐私政策处理服务数据。

网页和桌面版本

这些版本使用 Unity 匿名身份验证和 Unity Leaderboards，处理匿名玩家标识、公开呼号和符合条件的分数。Unity 可能为服务运行处理网络信息及诊断数据，详情见 Unity 游戏玩家与应用用户隐私政策。iOS 版不启动该 Unity 登录流程。

选择与保留

在游戏玩家账户页面选择游客，可以停止游戏内的 Game Center 排行榜和 iCloud 同步。切换身份会保留原有记录。删除游戏会移除本地数据，但不会自动删除 Apple 保存的分数和云存档；重新安装并连接后可能恢复。

Game Center 和 iCloud 账户由 Apple 管理，游戏不创建独立账户。Apple 服务的数据和账户管理请通过 Apple 数据与隐私门户或 Apple 支持。我们无法访问你的私人 iCloud 存档，也无法承诺通过客服邮件删除 Apple 管理的记录。游戏数据问题可联系我们；记录保存在设备或服务提供方，直至通过适用方式移除。

客服与本网站

向客服发邮件时，我们会收到你的邮箱及邮件中提供的信息，仅用于回答和处理请求，并仅在客服和适用义务所需期间保留。你可通过下方联系方式提出访问或删除请求。本网站无广告或分析脚本；托管服务商可能为网页运行和安全处理标准请求信息。语言切换仅在浏览器保存语言偏好。

儿童

游戏未提交至 App Store 儿童类别，不要求玩家填写年龄或联系方式。可选 Apple 服务受其账户及家庭控制管理。如果儿童向客服发送了个人信息，请联系我们申请移除。
