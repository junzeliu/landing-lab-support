Need a hand with your landing?

Email justinliu.photo@gmail.com. Include your device model, iOS version, mission and a short description. A screenshot can help. Never send passwords or verification codes.

How do I control the car?

Play in landscape. Press LIFT to start at 50% power; slide up or down through 0–100% and release to stop. AIR JETS aims the vapor using your camera view, pushing the car in the opposite direction. Switch to TILT to rotate the car.

Where should I land?

The amber ring is your landing target. In Car Jump and Truck Convoy, clear the required gates before landing. Red gates show an unsuccessful clearance.

Why did I run out of air?

All thrusters share a limited tank. Use short corrections and save air for the final descent. Mission 02, Running on Empty, teaches air management.

Can I practice any mission?

All twelve campaign missions are selectable. Replay Flight School and the opening film from the preflight menu. Manual Roll is the final and hardest mission.

Game Center and guest play

Choose Play as guest for local play without signing in. Choose Game Center for mission leaderboards. You can change your choice in Menu → Presentation → Player account. Game Center uses the account configured in your device Settings.

How do I restore my progress?

Install the game on the other device and use the same Game Center account and the same iCloud account on both. Choose Game Center in the game, connect to the internet and check the iCloud status in Player account. Medals, best scores, paint and tutorial progress synchronize when iCloud is available. Guest saves stay on the device; switching profiles does not delete earlier records. Language and sound are device-specific.

Does the game need internet?

Gameplay and local progress work offline. Rankings and iCloud sync need a connection. Eligible scores are queued locally when uploads cannot complete.

Language and sound

English and Simplified Chinese follow your device language initially. Choose your language and sound preferences in Menu → Presentation.

Is it free?

Yes. The first release is free with no ads or in-app purchases. Designed for iPhone in landscape; iPad runs the iPhone compatibility layout.

着陆遇到问题？

请发邮件至 justinliu.photo@gmail.com，注明设备型号、iOS 版本、任务名称和问题描述，可以附截图。请勿发送密码或验证码。

如何操控跑车？

横屏游玩。按下升力按钮时从 50% 功率开始，上下滑动可调整至 0–100%，松开即停止。空气喷口控制按摄像机视角决定喷气方向，跑车向相反方向移动；切换姿态控制可翻滚跑车。

该降落在哪里？

琥珀色目标圈是着陆目标。跃车和卡车车队任务还需要先通过指定关卡线；红色表示未成功通过。

为什么空气用完了？

所有喷口共用有限气罐。用短促调整，并为最后的下降保留空气。任务 02「气罐告急」帮助你学习空气管理。

可以直接练习任意任务吗？

12 项战役任务均可选择。可在起飞前菜单重玩操作教程和开场动画；手动翻滚是最后、也是最难的任务。

Game Center 与游客

选择游客即可在本机游玩，无需登录。选择 Game Center 可使用任务排行榜。可在菜单 → 表现设置 → 玩家账户中重新选择。Game Center 使用设备设置中的账户。

如何在另一台设备恢复存档？

两台设备安装游戏，并使用相同的 Game Center 账户和相同的 iCloud 账户。在游戏里选择 Game Center，连接网络，并在玩家账户页面查看 iCloud 状态。iCloud 可用时会同步奖牌、最高分、车漆和教程进度。游客存档仅保存在本机；切换身份不会删除原有记录。语言和声音设置各设备独立保存。

需要联网吗？

游戏和本地进度可离线使用。排行榜和 iCloud 同步需要网络；无法上传时，符合条件的分数会在本地等待重试。

语言和声音

初次运行默认跟随设备语言，可在菜单 → 表现设置中切换简体中文 / English，以及调整声音。

免费吗？

首发免费，无广告、无应用内购买。为横屏 iPhone 设计；iPad 使用 iPhone 兼容布局运行。
